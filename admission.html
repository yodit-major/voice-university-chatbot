AAU ADMISSION

Addis Ababa University

How to apply

1

Get a Wereda Support Letter

Obtain the required support letter from your Wereda before continuing with your application.

2

Fill in the Application Form

Provide your personal information, educational background and preferred field of study.

3

Take the UAT

Complete the University Admission Test (UAT) as part of the admission process.

4

Admission Decision

After completing the requirements, wait for the university's admission result.

Important

Admission requirements and procedures may change. Check the latest instructions from the official university or relevant admission authority.

HAWASSA ADMISSION

Hawassa University

You may be allocated to Hawassa University

Students may be allocated to Hawassa University based on the applicable university placement and admission system. Whether a separate application is needed depends on your student category and the current admission requirements.

How the allocation works

1

Complete Your Information

Make sure your academic and personal information is complete and accurate.

2

Complete the Required Process

Follow the admission and placement requirements applicable to your student category.

3

Wait for Placement

Your result will determine whether you are allocated to Hawassa University or another institution.

4

Check Your Result

Check your placement result and follow the university's registration instructions.

Important

Admission and placement requirements can change. Always verify the latest requirements with the relevant university or official admission authority.
