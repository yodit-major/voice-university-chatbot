11 TIKIMT
CULTURE & MUSIC

Legendary Art Night

A special 61st evening celebrating the legendary Ethiopian music artist Abegaz Kibrework Shiyota and his contribution to Ethiopia's musical history.

The evening will feature music, poetry, storytelling, criticism, dance and a fashion show, with celebrated singers performing in his honor.

Wednesday, Tikimt 11, 2019 E.C.
11:30 AM
AAU Cultural Center, 6 Kilo Campus
One evening, many stories. One stage, a thousand emotions.
